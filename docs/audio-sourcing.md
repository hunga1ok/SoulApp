# Audio sourcing register

Updated: 2026-10-05

## Release rule

An audio file becomes playable only after the repository records its source,
creator, download date, exact licence, proof of that licence, and internal
approval. `pending_asset` files stay visible in Explore but cannot play.

Do not describe any frequency track as healing, changing energy, or producing
a financial or relationship outcome. Labels such as 432 Hz, 528 Hz, and 888 Hz
describe a spiritual/wellness listening category only.

## Source options

| Source | Suitable content | Use rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Freesound CC0](https://freesound.org/help/faq/) | Rain, ocean, fireplace, room tone and other nature beds | Select only individual files labelled CC0. Preserve the source URL and author in the rights record. |
| [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/search/432hz/) | Ambient, meditation and frequency-themed music candidates | Pixabay is royalty-free, not copyright-free. Confirm the asset page and licence at download; do not redistribute the file as a standalone audio product. |
| Original commission | Guided voice, music, sound baths | Keep the written assignment of commercial app rights, master files, and creator agreement. |
